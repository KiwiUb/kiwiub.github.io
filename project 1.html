"Music Icons" book series cover design

The “Music Idols” book series was created to bring attention to the multi-disciplinary life of some of the most recognized musicians of the modern world by presenting a diverse collection of novels, poetry and theatre works published by those artists. That said, the intention behind the design of its covers seeks to transmit that idea in a visual manner, while also being highly recognizable by the target audience.

Discover the world inside the mind of your favourite artist.

What does this work mean to the author?

The back cover of the book holds a quote from the author that shows the artist’s feelings or intention for the work.
