Modern Walker

A well spread problem is the main focus of this product: The elders are often refusing to use their medical aids, such as walkers, in fear of not being taken seriously, or seeming ill. Moreover, the “ugly” and often industrial look of the walkers available at the market does not help the cause. This walker seeks to appeal to that public by offering a more modern look, and elevated comfortability of use.

Not a medical aid - a sport/wellbeing gadget

The angulated forms and professional finishes simulate the ones of a bicycle.

Aesthetic appeal

The silhouette of this walker imitates, in a minimalistic way, the shape of a butterfly’s wings.

No unnecessarily big gadgets at home

The book-like spread design makes putting away the walker easier than ever
